# Administrator Guide

## Current QA status

This guide describes the current V2 application. It is not a claim that every administrator workflow has passed a signed-in client acceptance test. The application uses the existing Supabase project and role scope; never share accounts or use another administrator's session to work around a permission denial.

## Getting started

1. Open the approved Golden Baobab HR V2 URL and sign in with the account issued to you.
2. Confirm the company and branch context shown in the application before entering or reviewing records.
3. Use only companies and branches assigned to your account. If an expected company or action is missing, request an administrator to review your membership; do not use another user's credentials.
4. Sign out on shared devices.

## Administrative work

Use Companies and Branches to create or update the business structure only if those actions are available to your assigned role. Use Users & Permissions for authorized role assignments. Employee, salary, attendance, leave and payroll visibility remains permission-scoped.

Before inviting users, confirm the recipient, company, branch and role. Invitation delivery and account activation have not been independently verified in this QA pass. Do not treat a queued invitation as proof that the recipient received or activated an account.

## Data protection

NRCs, statutory identifiers, compensation, bank details and private employee documents are sensitive. Access them only for an authorized work purpose. Exported payroll and bank files must be stored in an approved private location and removed according to the client's retention policy. Do not send them through public links.

## Escalation

Report a missing company, unexpected access, failed save or permission denial to the system administrator. Include the page, action and non-sensitive error text; do not attach employee identifiers, salary details, credentials or session tokens to general support messages.
